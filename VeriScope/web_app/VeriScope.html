Dataset
Train on the real LIAR dataset (or your own)

Upload LIAR train.tsv (+ valid.tsv, test.tsv) or a CSV/TSV with label,text per line. A file named “test” is used as the test set; otherwise 25% is held out.

Pipeline

Cleaning → lower-casing, URL/symbol removal, numbers → num, negations kept → stop-word removal → light stemming → TF-IDF (unigrams + bigrams, sublinear tf, L2) → Multinomial Naive Bayes and Logistic Regression (class-balanced SGD) → Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1 on a held-out test set. The better model by macro-F1 is used for predictions; word highlights come from Logistic Regression weights.

Limitations. The model learns writing patterns, not facts – it cannot verify whether a claim is true. The built-in sample is tiny and hand-written, so it only recognises sensational vs measured style. For genuine results, train on LIAR (typical text-only accuracy is modest: roughly mid-20s % for 6 classes, low 60s % for Fake vs Credible).

Real-world use: triage for fact-checkers, newsroom tools, moderation support, media-literacy teaching.
